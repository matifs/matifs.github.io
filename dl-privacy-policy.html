Privacy Policy for DriveLedger

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

DriveLedger ("DriveLedger", "we", "our", or "us") is a vehicle and driving utility application that provides features such as driving speed monitoring, trip distance and cost tracking, fuel calculations, maintenance reminders, logbook records, driving statistics, and performance testing.

This Privacy Policy explains what information DriveLedger may access, how that information is used and stored, and your choices regarding the information used by the application.

1. Information DriveLedger Uses

DriveLedger may access or process the following categories of information depending on the features you use.

1.1 Location Information

DriveLedger uses your device's location services and GPS to provide driving-related features.

Location information may be used to:

  • Calculate driving speed.
  • Calculate trip distance.
  • Calculate total distance and odometer information.
  • Record GPS accuracy.
  • Display latitude and longitude.
  • Display altitude.
  • Display bearing or direction.
  • Track driving while the application is running.
  • Continue driving tracking when the application is running in the background, when the required Android permissions have been granted.
  • Perform driving performance tests.

Location access is required because GPS information is an essential part of DriveLedger's driving and performance features.

DriveLedger does not intentionally use your precise GPS location for advertising or advertising personalization.

1.2 Driving and Vehicle Information

DriveLedger may store information generated while you use the application, including:

  • Trip distance.
  • Total distance and odometer values.
  • Driving speed.
  • Fuel consumption settings.
  • Fuel price settings.
  • Fuel cost calculations.
  • Selected distance, speed, volume, and currency units.
  • Engine oil reminder information.
  • Gear oil reminder information.
  • Maintenance records.
  • Notes entered by the user.
  • Performance test results.
  • Performance test date and time.
  • 0-60 mph or 0-100 km/h acceleration results.
  • 1/4-mile performance results.
  • 1/2-mile performance results.
  • Recorded top speed.
  • Distance measured during performance tests.

This information is used to provide the features and calculations available in DriveLedger.

1.3 Information You Enter

You may voluntarily enter information such as:

  • Vehicle-related notes.
  • Maintenance notes.
  • Fuel prices.
  • Fuel economy or average consumption.
  • Odometer values.
  • Other application settings.

DriveLedger does not require an account or password for its core functionality.

2. How Your Driving Data Is Stored

DriveLedger is designed to store driving settings, logbook information, maintenance information, and performance records locally on your Android device.

Unless a particular feature explicitly states otherwise, DriveLedger does not upload your driving records, GPS history, logbook entries, or performance history to a DriveLedger-operated server.

If you uninstall DriveLedger, locally stored application data may be removed by Android.

You are responsible for maintaining your own backups if you need to preserve locally stored information.

3. Background Location

Some DriveLedger features require driving tracking to continue while the application is not visible on the screen.

For example, you may start a drive and then use another application while DriveLedger continues tracking the trip.

When background tracking is enabled and the appropriate Android permissions have been granted, DriveLedger may continue accessing location information in the background for driving-related functionality.

Background tracking is intended to operate only while the relevant driving/tracking functionality is active.

You can control location permissions through your Android device settings.

4. Advertising

DriveLedger currently does not display advertisements and does not currently integrate Google AdMob or another advertising SDK.

DriveLedger therefore does not currently use an advertising ID for advertising purposes.

If advertising services are introduced in a future version of DriveLedger, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant advertising data collection and processing before or when those features are introduced.

5. Third-Party Services

DriveLedger may use third-party Android or Google services required for application functionality.

Such services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

DriveLedger does not currently integrate an advertising service such as Google AdMob.

6. Device and Technical Information

DriveLedger itself does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, SMS messages, phone call contents, or personal photographs for its core driving functionality.

Android and third-party services may independently process technical information required to operate their services.

7. Information DriveLedger Does Not Intentionally Collect

DriveLedger does not intentionally collect the following information for its own purposes:

  • Passwords.
  • Contacts.
  • SMS messages.
  • Phone call contents.
  • Photos or personal media.
  • Microphone recordings.
  • Email contents.
  • Banking credentials.
  • Credit or debit card numbers.

8. Data Sharing

DriveLedger does not sell your personal information or driving records to third parties.

DriveLedger does not currently operate a cloud service that uploads or stores your driving history, GPS history, logbook records, or performance records on DriveLedger servers.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, court order, or governmental request.

9. Data Security

DriveLedger takes reasonable measures to protect information stored by the application.

Driving records and application settings are primarily stored locally on your Android device.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should protect your Android device using appropriate security measures such as a screen lock and current operating-system security updates.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Because DriveLedger primarily stores driving records and settings locally on your device, those records generally remain on the device until you delete them using available application functionality, clear the application's data, uninstall DriveLedger, or otherwise remove the stored application data through Android.

DriveLedger does not currently maintain a server-side copy of your local driving records or performance history.

11. Children's Privacy

DriveLedger is not specifically designed for children.

DriveLedger does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children for its own purposes.

12. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your Android version and applicable laws, you may control application permissions through your Android device settings.

These controls may include:

  • Location permission.
  • Background location permission.
  • Notification permission.
  • Other permissions requested by future versions of the application.

You may revoke permissions through Android settings. Revoking required permissions may prevent certain DriveLedger features from functioning.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when DriveLedger's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published at the privacy policy URL associated with DriveLedger.

The Effective Date at the beginning of this policy indicates when the current version became effective.
