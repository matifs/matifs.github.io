# Privacy Policy for DriveLedger **Effective Date:** September 20, 2026 DriveLedger ("DriveLedger", "we", "our", or "us") is a vehicle and driving utility application that provides features such as driving speed monitoring, trip distance and cost tracking, fuel calculations, maintenance reminders, logbook records, performance testing, and driving statistics. This Privacy Policy explains what information DriveLedger may access or collect, how that information is used, how advertising services may process information, and what choices you have. By using DriveLedger, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. ## 1. Information DriveLedger Uses DriveLedger may access or process the following categories of information depending on the features you use. ### 1.1 Location Information DriveLedger uses your device's location services and GPS to provide driving-related features. Location information may be used to: * Calculate driving speed. * Calculate trip distance. * Calculate total distance/odometer information. * Record GPS accuracy. * Display latitude and longitude. * Display altitude. * Display bearing/direction. * Track driving while the app is running. * Continue driving tracking when the app is running in the background, when the required permissions have been granted. * Perform performance tests such as acceleration and distance measurements. DriveLedger requires location access because accurate location information is an essential part of its driving and performance features. Where Android requires background location access for continued tracking, DriveLedger may request the appropriate Android permission. Android controls when and how this permission can be granted. DriveLedger does not intentionally use your GPS location for advertising purposes. Google's advertising policies require appropriate disclosure and consent when precise location information is collected or disclosed for advertising or related purposes. DriveLedger's driving functionality uses location for the features described above rather than intentionally providing your precise GPS location to advertisers. ### 1.2 Driving and Vehicle Information DriveLedger may store information generated while you use the application, including: * Trip distance. * Total distance/odometer value. * Driving speed. * Fuel consumption settings. * Fuel price settings. * Fuel cost calculations. * Selected distance, speed, volume, and currency units. * Engine oil reminder information. * Gear oil reminder information. * Maintenance records. * Notes entered by you. * Performance test results. * Performance test date and time. * Acceleration results such as 0–60 mph or 0–100 km/h. * 1/4-mile and 1/2-mile test results. * Recorded top speed. * Distance measured during performance tests. These records are used to provide the features of DriveLedger. ### 1.3 Information You Enter You may voluntarily enter information such as: * Vehicle-related notes. * Maintenance notes. * Fuel prices. * Fuel economy/average consumption. * Odometer values. * Other settings required for calculations. DriveLedger does not require you to create an account to use its core functionality. ## 2. How Your Driving Data Is Stored DriveLedger is designed to store driving settings, logbook information, maintenance information, and performance records locally on your Android device. Unless a particular feature explicitly states otherwise, DriveLedger does not upload your driving records, GPS history, logbook entries, or performance history to a DriveLedger-operated server. If you uninstall the application, locally stored application data may be removed by Android. You are responsible for maintaining your own backups if you need to preserve locally stored information. ## 3. Background Location Some DriveLedger features require driving tracking to continue while the application is not visible on the screen. For example, you may start a drive and then use another application while DriveLedger continues tracking the trip. When background tracking is enabled and the appropriate Android permissions have been granted, DriveLedger may continue accessing location information in the background for driving-related functionality. Background tracking is not intended to collect location information when you have stopped using DriveLedger's tracking functionality. You can control location permissions through Android device settings. ## 4. Advertising DriveLedger may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. Advertising providers may collect or process certain information from your device in order to: * Deliver advertisements. * Measure advertisement performance. * Limit repeated advertisements. * Detect and prevent fraud and abuse. * Provide personalized advertising where permitted and applicable. * Provide non-personalized or contextual advertising where applicable. Depending on the advertising configuration, applicable law, your device settings, and your consent choices, advertising-related information may include information such as: * Advertising identifiers. * IP address. * Device and app information. * Approximate location or coarse geographic information. * Information about advertisement interactions. * Diagnostic and performance information related to advertising. * Information used for fraud prevention and security. * Information used to measure advertising effectiveness. Google explains that personalized advertising can use information such as previous activity, interests, demographics, and other advertising-related signals. Users can control certain advertising and personalization settings through Google's and Android's privacy controls. DriveLedger does not intentionally send your precise GPS coordinates, driving history, logbook records, or performance records to advertisers for personalized advertising. Advertising providers may nevertheless process information according to their own privacy policies and the configuration of the advertising services used by DriveLedger. For information about Google's handling of data in advertising services, please review Google's privacy and advertising documentation. ## 5. Personalized and Non-Personalized Advertising Depending on your location, applicable privacy requirements, consent choices, and the advertising configuration used by DriveLedger, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized. Personalized advertisements may use information associated with advertising identifiers or other permitted signals to make advertisements more relevant. Non-personalized advertisements do not use a user's previous activity for personalized ad targeting, although advertising technology may still process information necessary to deliver advertisements, measure advertising, maintain frequency limits, and prevent fraud or abuse. Where required, DriveLedger and its advertising partners may provide consent or privacy controls for applicable users. You may also control certain advertising preferences through your Android device and Google account settings. ## 6. Device and Technical Information Third-party services used by DriveLedger, particularly advertising services, may automatically process certain technical information from your device. Depending on the services and configuration in use, this may include: * Android device information. * Operating system information. * App version. * Advertising identifier. * IP address. * Network information. * Approximate geographic information. * Diagnostic information. * Advertisement interaction information. This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and service operation. ## 8. Information DriveLedger Does Not Intentionally Collect DriveLedger does not intentionally collect the following information for its own purposes: * Passwords. * Contacts. * SMS messages. * Phone call contents. * Photos or personal media. * Microphone recordings. * Email contents. * Banking credentials. * Credit/debit card numbers. DriveLedger does not require an account or password for its core driving features. ## 9. Data Sharing DriveLedger does not sell your personal information or driving records to third parties. DriveLedger may allow information to be processed by third-party service providers when necessary for services used by the application, such as advertising. Third-party advertising providers may independently collect and process advertising-related information as described in their own privacy policies. DriveLedger may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, court order, or governmental request. ## 10. Data Security DriveLedger takes reasonable measures to protect information stored by the application. Driving records and application settings are primarily stored locally on your Android device. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect your Android device using appropriate security measures such as a screen lock and current operating-system security updates. ## 11. Data Retention and Deletion Because DriveLedger primarily stores driving records and settings locally on your device, those records generally remain on the device until you: * Delete them using available application functionality; * Clear the application's data; * Uninstall DriveLedger; or * Otherwise remove the stored application data through Android. Third-party advertising providers may retain advertising-related information according to their own policies and retention practices. If DriveLedger introduces account-based or server-based data storage in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data retention and deletion procedures. ## 12.
