1. Data Collection & Privacy First Approach

Zero Developer Data Access: We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, location coordinates, or user data on external servers. All mock location operations and settings remain 100% local on your device.

Specifically:

  • Location Data: The mock location data generated by this app is created and processed locally on your device. We have no access to your real location or your mocked location history.
  • User Inputs & Favorites: Any saved locations, search histories, or preferences stay exclusively in your device's local memory or app storage.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Device Identifier Collection

To support free development, Mock Location uses third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to display advertisements within the application.

While the developer does not collect any personal data, third-party ad networks may automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable technical information from your device, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (e.g., Google AAID), unique Device IDs, or hardware identifiers.
  • Technical Information: Device model, operating system version, screen size, IP address, and network connection type.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Information regarding ad impressions, clicks, and performance metrics.

This information is collected directly by third-party ad SDKs solely for the purpose of serving relevant ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and analytics. For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review:

  • Google Advertising & Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

3. How You Can Control Data & Ads

You have full control over your device data and advertising preferences:

  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising identifiers at any time via your device settings:
    Settings → Google → Ads → Delete / Reset Advertising ID
  • Local Data Removal: You can erase all app settings and saved data by clearing the app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe an ad network has inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.
