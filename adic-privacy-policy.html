Privacy Policy

Android Device Info Check respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

By using Android Device Info Check, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Collected

Android Device Info Check may access device-related information directly on your device in order to display system information.

The app may access:

  • Device manufacturer and model
  • Android version and system information
  • CPU and GPU information
  • RAM and storage details
  • Battery information
  • Screen information
  • WiFi and Bluetooth status information

Permissions

Some app features require Android permissions.

  • Location Permission
    Used only to access WiFi SSID information on modern Android devices.
  • Bluetooth Permission
    Used only to display Bluetooth device status and related information.

Data Collection

Android Device Info Check does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers.

All device information displayed by the app is processed locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

Future versions of the app may include third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertisements. These services may collect limited anonymous data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

The app is designed to minimize data access and does not intentionally share device information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Android Device Info Check is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
