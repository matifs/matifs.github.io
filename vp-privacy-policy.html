# Privacy Policy **Last updated:** June 26, 2026 Thank you for using **Video Player (with PiP)**. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the app and how it is used. We are committed to protecting your privacy. The app does **not** upload, store, or share your personal videos or files on our servers. ## 1. Information We Collect ### Information collected by the app The app stores certain information **locally on your device** to provide its features, including: * Recent stream URLs * Saved RTSP camera profiles * Playback position * Subtitle and audio track preferences * App theme and other settings * Ad-free purchase status This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us. ### Information collected by third-party services The app uses trusted Google services that may automatically collect limited information, including: * Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID, when available) * App usage statistics * App performance and diagnostics * Crash reports * Purchase verification for Google Play Billing * Basic device information used for analytics and advertising This information is collected by Google's services in accordance with their own privacy policies. ## 2. Permissions Used The app may request the following permissions: * **Photos and Videos / Storage access** – to browse, play, move, and delete your video files. * **Internet access** – required for: * Playing network video streams * Connecting to RTSP/IP cameras * Displaying advertisements * Google Play Billing * Firebase Analytics * **Picture-in-Picture (PiP)** – allows videos to continue playing while using other apps. These permissions are used only to provide the requested features. ## 3. Advertising The app displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. Google may use device identifiers and other information to provide and measure advertisements. Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites You may also manage your advertising preferences through your Google account settings. ## 4. Analytics The app uses **Google Firebase Analytics** to better understand how the app is used. Analytics helps us improve the app by providing anonymous information such as: * Number of users * Feature usage * Session duration * Device type * App version * General performance information We do **not** use Firebase Analytics to access your personal videos, documents, passwords, or RTSP credentials. ## 5. Google Play Billing If you purchase the optional **Ad-Free** upgrade, payments are securely processed by **Google Play**. We do **not** receive or store your payment card information. Google handles all payment processing and purchase verification. ## 6. Video Files and Camera Streams Your videos, subtitles, audio tracks, stream URLs, and RTSP camera feeds are processed directly on your device. We do **not** upload, record, store, or monitor: * Your videos * Camera feeds * RTSP credentials * Stream URLs * Personal media files ## 7. Data Security We do not maintain servers that store your personal content. Data stored by the app (such as settings and saved camera profiles) remains on your device. ## 8. Children's Privacy The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. ## 9. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page together with the updated revision date. ## 10.
